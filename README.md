# Digital Health Technologies for Dementia Care: A Systematic Literature Review

## 📌 Overview
This systematic literature review investigates the integration of continuous digital monitoring, ambient intelligence, and objective digital phenotyping for early detection and longitudinal health assessment in dementia care[cite: 1]. You can read or download the full paper [here](https://github.com/alessandratolomelli/dementia-digital-health-literature-review/blob/main/docs/Digital%20Health%20Technologies%20for%20Dementia%20Care.pdf).

## 🛠️ Technological Taxonomy
Our review categorizes solutions into three main pillars:
1. **Wearable Devices:** High-frequency movement and physiological tracking (IMUs, consumer smartwatches, wearable EEG).
2. **Smart-Home & Ambient Sensing:** Non-intrusive in-home monitoring (PIR sensors, magnetic door contacts) to establish Activities of Daily Living (ADL) baselines.
3. **Predictive Algorithms:** Analytical frameworks (such as deep learning and hybrid models like *HEALNET*) to model disease trajectories.

## ⚙️ Key Engineering Challenges
* **Synthetic vs. Real-World Data:** Heavy reliance on controlled benchmarks (CASAS) or synthetic simulations (*NeuroSense*).
* **Domain Shift & Adherence:** Vulnerability to unconstrained home environments and compliance barriers caused by cognitive decline.
* **Data Privacy:** Need for Federated Learning (FL) and Differential Privacy to comply with GDPR.
